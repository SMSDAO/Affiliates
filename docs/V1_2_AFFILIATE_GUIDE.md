### 1. Getting Started
1. **Enrollment**: Complete the public registration form (`/affiliate-register`), declare your promotional traffic sources, select your preferred currency, and accept FTC disclosure requirements.
2. **Dedicated Portal Access**: Access your personalized portal (`/affiliate-portal`) immediately upon approval.
3. **Primary Referral Link**: Share your branded URL (`https://aff.ai/r/YOUR_CODE`) across video descriptions, blogs, and social bio links.
4. **Offline & Stream Promotion**: Generate and download vector SVG and 300 DPI PNG QR codes directly from the portal.
5. **Coupons**: Request custom checkout coupons (e.g. `YOURNAME20`) that attribute sales without requiring link clicks.

### 2. Multi-Tier Overrides & Ranks
- **Rank Milestones**: Advance automatically through Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond tiers. Higher ranks unlock up to 1.5x commission multipliers and milestone cash bonuses.
- **Sub-Affiliate Recruits**: Earn 5% override commissions on Level 2 sales generated by affiliates who register through your link.

### 3. Payouts & Thresholds
