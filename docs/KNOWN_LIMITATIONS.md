# KNOWN LIMITATIONS & OPERATIONAL BOUNDARIES

1. **Third-Party Network Approval Timelines**: While SUPER AFFILIATE MANAGER AI automates partner registration and generates compliant publisher profiles, actual affiliate network approvals (e.g. Rakuten Advertising, Amazon Associates tax verification) remain subject to human compliance review by the respective external merchants.
2. **Social Media Direct Publishing Access**: Direct automated social posting requires verified OAuth client credentials from TikTok, YouTube, and Meta developer apps. Where direct developer keys are awaiting app review, the system automatically provides formatted channel scripts and copy-paste workflows.
3. **Gaming Jurisdiction Licensing**: Promotions for licensed gaming operators are strictly bound by target jurisdiction laws. Unlicensed jurisdictions and unverified age brackets are blocked by the Compliance Agent.
