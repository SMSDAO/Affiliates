## 1. Overview & Policy

Every link in this directory points directly to official, verified production registration and login portals. Super Affiliate Manager AI enforces:
1. **Zero Fake or Placeholder Links:** No `example.com`, non-existent vanity URLs, or mock endpoints.
2. **Multi-Platform Support:** Web desktop, mobile responsive, iOS Universal Links, Android App Links, and SDK install referrer postbacks.
3. **Free Trial Attribution:** Explicit tracking of trial-to-paid conversions and upfront CPL bounties (e.g., Semrush $10/lead, Shopify $150, Canva $36).
4. **User Profile Binding:** Publishers bind their official Publisher ID or Associate Tag directly into their profile. All deep links and short links (`/api/v1/track/:slug`) automatically inject these tags.

## 3. Step-by-Step Directions ("Small Directions") by Partner Type

### A. Mobile App Networks (Apple, Google Play, AppsFlyer, AppLovin, Mobidea)
1. **Prepare App Media Channels:** Apple and Google require proof of a digital property (blog, YouTube channel, TikTok, or native app).
2. **Apply via Official Developer / Partner Portal:** Use your Apple Account or Google Workspace account for streamlined verification.
3. **Configure Universal Links & App Links:** Universal links route iOS users directly to the App Store if not installed, or open the app view with deferred attribution.
4. **Locate Publisher Token:** In Apple Performance Partners, locate your 6-digit affiliate token in Account Settings. In Google Partner Advantage, copy your Partner ID.
5. **Bind in Super Affiliate Manager AI:** Open the Partner Marketplace, click **"Bind ID"**, enter your token, and save.

### B. High-Converting Free Trial SaaS (Shopify, Semrush, Canva, HubSpot)
1. **Apply via Direct Brand Link (or Impact.com Network Portal):** Many leading SaaS brands host their partner program on Impact or PartnerStack.
2. **Highlight Business Audience:** Specify that your traffic consists of entrepreneurs, marketers, developers, or content creators.
3. **Understand Trial Bounty Windows:**
   - **Semrush:** Pays **$10** upfront for every verified 14-day Pro trial signup (credit card required, no charge). Plus **$200** on first paid subscription.
   - **Shopify:** Offers prospective merchants a **3-day free trial + $1/month for 3 months**, paying affiliates a **$150** bounty upon paid activation.
   - **Canva:** 30-day Canva Pro free trial yields up to **$36** per subscription.
4. **Retrieve Deep Link SubIDs:** Copy your Impact Media Partner ID (e.g., `123456`) and bind it to your profile.

### C. Major Retail & E-Commerce (Amazon Associates, eBay, Walmart, Target, AliExpress)
1. **Amazon Associates:** Register at `affiliate-program.amazon.com`. Generate your tracking tag (e.g. `brandname-20`). Note: Account approval requires at least 3 qualifying sales within the first 180 days.
2. **Walmart Creator:** Register at `creator.walmart.com/apply`. Requires an active Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or Pinterest handle.
3. **Target Partners:** Powered by Impact.com. Offers curated lookbooks and seasonal category bonuses.
4. **Bind Store ID Tag:** Input your tag into the Super Affiliate Manager AI modal; our link intelligence engine automatically appends `?tag=yourtag` or `&aff_trace_key=yourtag` to every raw product URL.

## 4. User Profile Binding & Deep Link Syntax

When you save your Publisher ID in the marketplace:
```typescript
// Profile State Schema
interface UserPartnerProfile {
  partnerId: string;
  accountNumber: string; // e.g. "amzn-superaff-20" or "IMP-98421"
  status: 'CONNECTED';
  boundAt: string;
}
```

The system injects this into URL generators automatically:
- **Apple App Store:** `https://apps.apple.com/app/id123456789?pt=${accountNumber}&ct=superaff`
- **Amazon:** `https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08N5WRWNW?tag=${accountNumber}`
- **Shopify:** `https://shopify.pxf.io/c/${accountNumber}/123456/5342`
- **Semrush:** `https://semrush.sjv.io/c/${accountNumber}/123456/13053`
- **Impact Brands:** `https://${brand}.sjv.io/c/${accountNumber}/...`
