## 1. Agency Capabilities Overview
The Agency & Reseller Suite allows marketing agencies and platform resellers to:
- Onboard and manage multiple client brand workspaces from a central dashboard.
- Assign dedicated Account Managers (e.g., Sarah Lin, Devon Reed) with scoped, role-based client access.
- Track client gross monthly spend, commission payouts, and net agency revenue share.
- Export itemized monthly billing reports and invoices (`.csv`) for accounting reconciliation.

---

## 2. Agency Scoped Roles
- **Agency Owner:** Unrestricted administrative clearance across all client workspaces and agency billing.
- **Account Manager:** Assigned to specific clients; manages partner programs, approved creatives, and support tickets.
- **Campaign Manager:** Scoped to creative asset generation, social publishing, and ad distribution.
- **Financial Manager:** Manages payout approvals, bank reconciliation, and client invoice generation.
- **Read-Only Analyst:** Read-only access to campaign analytics and funnel conversion rates.
