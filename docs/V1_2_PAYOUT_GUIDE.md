# SUPER AFFILIATE MANAGER AI ENTERPRISE
## V1.2.0 Payouts & Disbursement Rail Guide

### 1. Supported Disbursement Providers
- **Stripe Connect**: Direct deposit to domestic and international bank accounts in 40+ countries.
- **PayPal Payouts**: Instant mass disbursements via email or PayPal ID.
- **Paystack**: Payouts to African and global banking networks.
- **Bank Wire / SEPA**: Manual bank wire processing for large institutional balances ($250 min).
- **Exportable CSV**: Universal ledger formatted for third-party payroll engines.

### 2. Payout Lifecycle
- **Requested**: Affiliate triggers withdrawal above $50.00 threshold.
- **Pending Review**: Automatic fraud sentinel screening & tax document check.
- **Approved**: Administrator signs off on batch.
- **Paid**: Transaction reference token generated and committed to the audit ledger.
